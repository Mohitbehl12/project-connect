# Community Guidelines

Kinara is for adults looking for real connection. These guidelines apply to profiles, photos, messages, voice messages, calls and anything else you share. Breaking them can lead to removed content, paused features, suspension or a ban. They are part of our Terms of Service.

## Be yourself

- Use your real first name and age, and photos that clearly show you.
- One person, one account. No fake, duplicate, shared or business profiles.
- Don't pretend to be someone else, and don't use anyone else's photos.

## Be respectful

- No harassment, bullying, insults, hate or threats — including about anyone's religion, caste, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or body.
- "No" means no. If someone doesn't reply, declines a call, unmatches or blocks you, don't try to reach them another way.
- Don't send sexual messages, photos or voice notes that weren't clearly welcome.

## Keep it safe

- Never ask for money, gifts, investments, loans, codes, PINs, card, bank or ID details. Report anyone who does.
- Don't share links to suspicious sites or apps, and don't pressure people to move to other apps.
- Don't share anyone's private information — their address, workplace, phone number or photos — without their permission.
- Never share, or threaten to share, intimate images of anyone. This is a crime in many countries and we report it.
- No content that promotes violence, self-harm, illegal drugs, weapons or other illegal activity.

## Adults only — zero tolerance

Kinara is for people 18 and over. Any content that sexualises minors, any attempt to contact or groom a minor, or any sexual exploitation leads to an immediate ban and a report to the authorities. If you think someone is under 18, report them.

## Photos

Allowed: clear photos of you. Not allowed: nudity or sexual content, violence or weapons, other people without their consent, children on their own, text or QR codes with contact details, or photos you don't have the right to use.

## Calls

Calls only happen after the other person accepts your call request. Don't record calls without consent, and end a call if you're asked to.

## Reporting and what we do

Report anything that worries you from the profile, chat or call screen. Reporting is confidential: the other person isn't told who reported them. We review reports — the most serious first — and may remove content, warn, restrict, suspend or ban accounts. If you disagree with a decision about you, you can appeal.
