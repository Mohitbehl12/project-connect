# Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Cancelling

You can cancel a subscription at any time in Settings › My subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing more is charged.

## When you get a refund

- **Charged in error** (for example charged twice, or charged after cancelling): full refund of the wrong charge.
- **Service unavailable** for a long period because of us: a refund or extra free days, your choice.
- **We end your account** for a reason other than you breaking our terms: refund of the unused part of your period.
- **Legal cooling-off rights:** where the law where you live gives you a right to cancel a new subscription within a set number of days (for example 14 days in the UK and EU), you get a refund as that law requires — which may be reduced for the part of the service you already used.
- Otherwise, payments for a period that has started are not refundable, but you can cancel so you aren't charged again.

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google under their own policies.

## How to ask

Write to {{supportEmail}} with the email on your account and the payment date. We reply within 7 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days depending on your bank.

## Refunds and bans

If we ban an account for serious breaches of our terms (for example fraud or harassment), unused time is not refunded, unless the law requires it or the ban is overturned on appeal.
