# Subscription & Payment Terms

These terms explain Kinara memberships: what they cost, free periods, renewal, cancellation and taxes. They are part of our Terms of Service. The Refund Policy explains refunds.

## 1. What you see before you pay

Before any payment, the checkout shows: the plan, the price and currency, the length of the period, whether it renews automatically and how often, any free period or discount and when it ends, and that taxes are included where that applies. Nothing is charged until you confirm.

## 2. Membership in India (gender-based pricing)

- **Women** (and everyone who is not a man): all membership features are free for life. No payment details are needed.
- **Men:** a 15-day free trial of Premium starts when you finish setting up your profile — once per person and device. No payment details are needed for the trial, and **nothing is charged automatically when it ends**. We remind you before the trial ends. To keep Premium afterwards, you choose a plan: ₹100 per month, or a longer plan at the price shown in the app.
- This pricing is shown before you finish signing up. If you change the gender on your profile, the pricing for your new gender applies from then on; a change from man to woman needs a completed selfie check first, to prevent misuse.

Pricing in other countries is shown in the app and may differ.

## 3. Renewal

- Monthly and longer plans you buy on our website renew automatically at the end of each period at the price you agreed, until you cancel. We remind you {{reminderDays}} days before each renewal charge.
- If a renewal payment fails, you keep access for a short grace period while the payment provider retries; we tell you what to do.
- If we change a price, we tell you in advance and the new price applies only from your next period after the notice. You can cancel before then.

## 4. Cancelling

Cancel any time in Settings › My subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. Deleting your account also cancels a website subscription. Subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play are managed and cancelled in your phone's subscription settings.

## 5. Taxes

Prices in India include GST. Elsewhere, the checkout shows whether taxes are included. A tax invoice is available for every payment.

## 6. Payment processing

Payments are handled by our payment provider (for example Razorpay). Your card, UPI or bank details go directly to them; Kinara never receives or stores them. Payment records are kept for 8 years because tax law requires it.

## 7. Offers and coupons

Offers and coupon terms are shown with the offer. One-time offers can be used once per person and device. We may refuse or reverse a discount obtained by misuse (for example multiple accounts).
