# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Kinara collects, why, how it is used and protected, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. {{company}} is responsible for your data (the "data fiduciary" or "controller"). Contact: {{privacyEmail}}.

## 1. The short version

- We collect what we need to run a safe dating service, and no more. We do not sell your data. Kinara shows a few clearly labelled **Sponsored** ads; advertisers never learn who you are (see section 5A).
- Other members see your profile — never your email, phone number, exact location or date of birth.
- Kinara uses your city, not GPS. Distances are rounded (for example "Within 10 km").
- Messages are encrypted on our servers, but they are **not end-to-end encrypted**: our safety team can read a conversation when it is reported.
- You can download your data, change your settings and consents, pause your profile or delete your account at any time in Privacy Center.

## 2. What we collect and why

**Account details** — email, phone number (if you add it), password (stored only as a one-way hash), date of birth. *Why:* to create and secure your account and to check you're old enough. *Basis:* performing our contract with you; legal obligation (age).

**Profile** — first name, gender, who you want to meet, city and country, photos, bio, interests, prompts, optional details such as height, job, education and lifestyle. *Why:* to show your profile and suggest matches. You choose what to add. *Basis:* contract; your consent for optional details.

**Personality answers** — your answers to our short questions. *Why:* compatibility suggestions. We never use them to guess sensitive characteristics.

**Location** — the city you choose, stored as an approximate point (about 1 km precision). We never collect GPS. *Why:* to show nearby people and rounded distances. You can hide your distance or city.

**Photos, selfie and media** — profile photos (location data removed when uploaded), the selfie you take for verification (deleted once a decision is made — we keep only the result), photos and voice messages you send in chat. *Why:* your profile, verification and chat. Photos are checked automatically for nudity and violence.

**Camera and microphone** — used only when you take a photo or selfie, record a voice message or join a call you accepted. The phone asks your permission first. Calls are not recorded.

**Messages and call information** — the messages you send (encrypted at rest), and for calls: who called whom, when, whether it was answered and how long it lasted. *Why:* to deliver chat and calls, investigate reports, and prevent abuse.

**Safety and security data** — reports you make or that are made about you, blocks, automated safety flags, risk signals (for example many accounts from one device), sign-in history (time, device type, IP address), and device identifiers. *Why:* to protect members, prevent fraud and keep accounts secure. *Basis:* legitimate interests / legal obligation.

**Payments** — what you bought, amounts, dates and invoices. Card, UPI and bank details go directly to our payment provider; we never see or store them.

**Consents and settings** — what you agreed to and when (for example terms versions, marketing), and your privacy settings.

**Device information** — device type, app version, push notification address, language. *Why:* to run the app and send notifications you turned on.

**Cookies** — see our Cookie Policy. We use only cookies needed to sign you in and keep the site secure.

We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 18.

## 3. How we use your data

- To run Kinara: your account, profile, matching, chat, calls, notifications and payments.
- To keep people safe: screening messages and photos, investigating reports, detecting fake accounts, scams and abuse, and enforcing our terms.
- To secure accounts: detecting unusual sign-ins and protecting against fraud.
- To communicate with you about your account, safety and changes to our terms. Marketing messages are sent only if you opted in, and you can opt out at any time.
- To meet legal obligations, such as tax records and responding to lawful requests.
- To improve Kinara using aggregated statistics.
- To choose which sponsored ads to show you (see section 5A).

## 4. Automated decisions and AI

We use automated tools to suggest matches, screen messages and photos for safety, and score fraud risk. These tools:

- never decide on their own to ban you — serious enforcement decisions are made by a person;
- may pause some features (for example new likes) on accounts with several risk signals until you complete the selfie check or a person reviews the account;
- never use or infer religion, caste, ethnicity, health, sexual orientation or political views;
- are logged, so you can see automated decisions about you in Privacy Center and appeal them.

Compatibility scores and conversation suggestions are estimates, labelled as such. A verified badge reflects a check at a point in time, not a guarantee.

## 5. Who can see your data

- **Other members** see your public profile only: first name, age, gender, city (or only country, if you choose), rounded distance (unless hidden), photos (the first only, if you choose "matches only"), bio, interests, prompts and verified badge. Matches see your messages and anything you send in chat.
- **Our staff** see what their role needs. For example, moderators see reported conversations; finance staff see payments but not messages. Staff use 2-step verification, and every view of private information is logged.
- **Service providers** process data for us under contract, only to provide their service — see the list below.
- **Authorities** — we disclose data when the law requires it (for example a valid court order or a lawful request from the police), or when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to prevent death or serious harm. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality in those cases. We check every request, disclose only what is needed, and record it.
- **If the business changes hands**, data may transfer to the new owner under this policy; we will tell you first.

## 5A. Advertising

Kinara shows a small number of ads, always labelled **Sponsored**. Ads never appear inside a conversation or during a call.

- **What we use to pick an ad:** your city and country, your age band, your device type and app language. If **personalised ads** are on, we also use your gender and the interests on your profile. We never use who you want to date, your relationship intent, your messages, your calls, your matches or likes, your photos, or anything sensitive (such as religion, health, sexuality or caste).
- **Personalised ads** are on by default where the law allows it, and off by default where your consent is needed first (for example the UK and EU). You can switch them off or on at any time in Privacy Center › Ads. With them off you still see ads, but they are less relevant.
- **What advertisers get:** only totals, such as how many people saw or tapped an ad and in which broad areas, with small numbers rounded so nobody can be singled out. They never receive your name, photos, profile, email, phone number, location, messages, call history or dating activity, and they cannot contact you through Kinara.
- **When you tap an ad**, you leave Kinara for the advertiser's website. We add a random click code to the link so the advertiser can tell us whether the visit led to a purchase or sign-up; the code does not identify you. What the advertiser's website collects is covered by its own privacy policy.
- **Your controls:** on any ad, tap ⋯ to hide it, report it, or see "Why am I seeing this?".

## 9. How we protect your data

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) everywhere; messages and sensitive fields encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); passwords hashed with Argon2id; optional 2-step verification; required 2-step verification and activity logs for staff; role-based access; rate limits and automated-account detection; tamper-evident audit logs; regular backups. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you can:

- **access** your data — Privacy Center › Download my data;
- **correct** it — edit your profile and settings, or contact us;
- **delete** it — Privacy Center › Delete my account (see what we keep and why in section 8);
- **port** it — the download is a machine-readable file;
- **withdraw consent** — Privacy Center › Your consents (this doesn't affect what we did before);
- **object to or restrict** some processing, and ask for a person to review an automated decision — use Appeal a decision or contact us;
- **nominate** someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (India);
- **complain** to our Grievance Officer, and to your data-protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board; in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your local authority).

We reply within {{grievanceReplyDays}} days, or sooner where the law requires.
