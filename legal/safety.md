# Safety Policy

This policy explains how Kinara works to keep members safe, what we expect from you, and how to get help. No service can remove every risk, but we design every feature with safety in mind.

## How Kinara protects you

- **Adults only:** date-of-birth check at sign-up and age rules per country; profiles reported as possibly under 18 are hidden while we check.
- **Consent first:** nobody can message you unless you both liked each other; nobody can call you unless you accept a call request; photos and voice messages unlock only after you've both written.
- **Privacy:** no GPS, rounded distances, your phone number and email are never shown, and calls go through Kinara.
- **Screening:** messages and photos are checked automatically for scams, threats, explicit images and risky links. You're warned about risky messages, and some links are blocked.
- **Selfie verification:** a check that your selfie matches your profile photos. It shows a badge; it does not prove identity or that someone is safe.
- **Fraud and abuse detection:** signals such as many accounts from one device or mass messaging are reviewed by people. Accounts with several signals may have new contact paused until they verify.
- **People review serious cases:** reports are prioritised by risk, and bans are decided by a person. You can appeal.

## Your tools

Block, report, mute a chat, unmatch, decline or turn off call requests, hide your distance or city, show your profile only to verified members, show extra photos only to matches, pause your profile, and delete your messages or account.

## Meeting in person

- Take your time and video call first if you'd like.
- Meet in a busy public place and arrange your own transport.
- Tell a friend where you're going and with whom; share your plans with them.
- Don't share your home address, workplace or financial details early.
- Leave if you feel uncomfortable — you never owe anyone your time.

## Emergencies

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (in India, 112). Kinara cannot send emergency help.

## Reporting and escalation

Report from the profile, chat or call screen, or write to {{safetyEmail}}. Each report gets a case number. Reports about threats, sexual misconduct, minors or immediate safety are reviewed first, and the account may be restricted while we investigate. Where there is a risk of serious harm, or the law requires it, we may share relevant information with the police or other authorities, and we preserve evidence under a legal hold. We cannot promise to keep everything confidential when the law requires disclosure.

## Law enforcement requests

We respond to valid legal requests from competent authorities. Each request is checked by a person, recorded, answered with only the data needed, and the relevant account is placed on a legal hold so evidence isn't deleted. Requests go to {{safetyEmail}}.
