# Cookie Policy

## What we use

Kinara's website uses only cookies that are strictly necessary:

- **Sign-in cookie** (pc_session): keeps you signed in. It is secure, not readable by scripts, and lasts up to 14 days or until you sign out.
- **Security**: protection against cross-site request forgery and abuse, using the same sign-in cookie and request checks.

The apps store sign-in tokens securely on your device (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android) instead of cookies. The home-screen web version of the app keeps its sign-in token in your browser's local storage on your device, and your notification and display settings there too. Nothing is shared with other websites.

## What we don't use

No advertising cookies, no third-party tracking cookies, and no cross-site tracking. Because we only use strictly necessary cookies, we don't show a cookie banner. If we ever add optional cookies (for example analytics), we will ask for your consent first, with no boxes pre-ticked, and update this policy.

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you won't be able to stay signed in.
