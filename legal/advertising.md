# Advertising Terms and Policy

These terms apply to businesses that advertise on Kinara ("you", the advertiser). They sit alongside our Terms of Service. {{company}} runs Kinara. Questions: {{supportEmail}}.

## 1. Who can advertise

- You must be a real business or professional, at least 18, able to enter into a contract, and allowed to sell what you advertise where you advertise it.
- Give accurate business details. We verify businesses before any ad goes live. We may ask for documents such as a GST certificate, a business registration or proof that you own the website.
- Verification levels are **Unverified**, **Verified business** and **Trusted advertiser**. A badge shows that we checked who you are at a point in time. **It is not a guarantee that a business, its products or its services are safe, good or lawful**, and members are told so.

## 2. How ads are reviewed

- Every ad is checked automatically and then reviewed by a person before it can run. We look at the images, video, text, website, business identity, category, targeting, claims and payment.
- If an ad is rejected we give a general reason, for example "Your advertisement could not be approved because it does not meet our advertising guidelines." You can edit it and submit it again.
- Changing a running ad's content, audience or placements sends it back for review.
- We may pause or remove any ad at any time, including after approval, if members report it or it breaks these rules.

## 3. Ads that are not allowed

- Fraud, scams, phishing, malware or "get rich quick" schemes, fake financial products or guaranteed returns.
- Illegal products or services, illegal drugs, weapons and explosives, counterfeit or "first copy" goods.
- Sexual services, escort services, explicit or sexually suggestive content, and anything involving minors.
- Gambling, betting and real-money gaming.
- Harassment, hate, extremist content, or anything that targets or excludes people for who they are.
- Misleading claims, fake reviews, impersonation, false urgency, or a website that doesn't match the ad.
- Spyware or apps for tracking a partner.
- Link shorteners, raw IP addresses, or websites that hide where they lead.

## 4. Regulated categories

Finance, health, alcohol and dating or relationship services need extra checks: licences where the law requires them, honest claims and suitable audiences. We may refuse them in some places or limit their placements.

## 5. Targeting and privacy

- You can choose locations (country, state or region, city), an age range of 18 or over, device and language. Gender and interests are used only for members who have personalised ads switched on.
- You can't target using sensitive information (such as religion, caste, health, sexuality, political views or financial hardship), who someone wants to date, or anything from their messages, calls or dating activity.
- You receive **only aggregated statistics**: impressions, unique reach, clicks, click-through rate, video views, conversions and spend. We never give you members' names, photos, profiles, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, messages, call history or dating activity.
- Conversion tracking uses a random click code. You must not try to combine it with other data to identify members, or use it for anything except measuring your campaign.

## 6. Budgets, prices and payment

- You pay in advance by adding balance. Payments are taken by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.
- Prices (per 1,000 views, per click, per action, or per day for a fixed placement) are shown before you submit, and may change for future spending with notice in your dashboard.
- Campaigns stop when the daily or total budget, or your balance, runs out. We do not guarantee any number of views, clicks, conversions or sales. Estimated reach is a rough guide only.
- Fixed placements are charged for the whole booked period when the ad is approved.
- Every top-up comes with a receipt that you can download from Billing. Amounts include GST where it applies.

## 7. Refunds

- You can ask for a refund of unused balance at any time from Billing. We review requests within 7 working days and refund to the original payment method.
- Money already spent on delivered views, clicks or actions, and fixed placements that have started, are not refundable, unless we failed to deliver what was booked.
- If we remove your ads or suspend your account for breaking these rules, we may hold unused balance while we investigate fraud or legal claims.

## 8. Your content

You confirm you own or have permission to use everything in your ads (including images of people, who must have agreed), and that your ads are truthful and lawful. You give us a licence to show, resize and format them on Kinara while your campaign runs.

## 9. Suspension

We may suspend an advertiser account that breaks these rules, gets repeated valid reports, gives false information, or puts members at risk. Suspension pauses all campaigns. You can appeal from the Appeal a decision page.
