# Anti-Harassment Policy (draft)

Kinara does not tolerate harassment, threats, stalking, impersonation, unwanted sexual content or intimate-image abuse.

## What counts

- Repeated contact after someone says no.
- Insults, slurs and bullying.
- Threats of violence.
- Blackmail and sextortion ("send money or I'll share your photos").
- Unwanted sexual messages or images.
- Sharing, or threatening to share, intimate images — real or fake/AI-made.
- Trying to find where someone lives or works.
- Pretending to be someone else.

In India these can also be crimes. The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 covers:
- sexual harassment (s.75);
- voyeurism (s.77);
- stalking (s.78);
- words or gestures insulting a woman's modesty (s.79);
- cheating by personation (s.319);
- criminal intimidation (s.351).

The IT Act 2000 covers:
- identity theft (s.66C);
- cheating by personation (s.66D);
- privacy violation (s.66E);
- obscene and sexually explicit material (ss.67, 67A).

Members are pointed to the police and cybercrime.gov.in / 1930. We never decide guilt under criminal law; we decide whether someone may keep using Kinara.

## What members can do (without confronting anyone)

- Report from a profile, a chat message or a call screen. The reported person is never told who reported them.
- Report and block in one step: the two people disappear from each other everywhere — Discover, likes, chat and calls.
- Unmatch, mute a chat, refuse call requests, and choose who can like them or see their profile.

## What the system does

- **Message screening.** Abuse triggers a kindness check before sending. Threats, sextortion and scam patterns open a report for a person. The receiver sees a warning next to the flagged message.
- **Calls.** Calls need consent: a request first, then accept. There is a cooldown after a decline and a stop after three declines.
- **Repeat offenders.** Several different reporters add a risk signal. Past the configured threshold the account is paused pending review — a person decides.

## Enforcement (decided by a person)

| Severity | Examples | Usual outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Threats, sextortion, intimate-image abuse, sexual misconduct, contact with a minor | Ban, evidence kept under legal hold, lawful disclosure on request |
| High | Harassment, impersonation | Suspension or ban |
| Medium | Unwanted contact, inappropriate content | Warning, content removed, then suspension if repeated |

Intimate images are removed from Kinara within 24 hours of a complaint (IT Rules 2021 r.3(2)(b)); our internal target is 12 hours.
