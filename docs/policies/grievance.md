# Grievance Redressal Policy (draft)

- **Who.** The Grievance Officer is named in Admin → Settings → Contacts and published on `/grievance` and in the Privacy Policy and Terms (IT Rules 2021 r.3(2); DPDP Act s.8(10), s.13).
- **How.** Anyone can file at `/grievance` — a member or not, signed in or not. Categories: account, content, intimate images or impersonation, personal data, payments, safety, other.
- **Acknowledgement.** Sent at once by email, with a reference number (GR-YYYYMM-NNNNNN). The legal limit is 24 hours.
- **Resolution deadlines:**
  - intimate images or impersonation: within 24 hours;
  - everything else: within 15 days (India's setting; other countries in Country rules).
  - Admin → Grievances shows the time left and turns red when overdue.
- **Privacy rights requests** (access, correction, erasure, withdraw consent, nominee).
  - A member names a nominee in Settings / Privacy Center (DPDP Act s.14).
  - A nominee who writes in must show proof (death certificate or evidence of incapacity, and their own identity) before staff act.
  - Every step is audited; the nominee's details never appear in the audit log. Most are self-serve in Privacy Center. A complaint about them is a grievance in the PRIVACY category. The DPDP Rules allow at most 90 days; our target is 15.
- **Escalation.** If not satisfied, the person may go to:
  - the Data Protection Board of India (personal data);
  - consumer forums;
  - the Grievance Appellate Committee (IT Rules r.3A).

  The resolution email names the route that fits the complaint: Data Protection Board for personal data; Grievance Appellate Committee (gac.gov.in, within 30 days) for content and account decisions; National Consumer Helpline (1915) for payments. Wording is a draft for the lawyer.
- **Records.** Contact details and complaint text are encrypted at rest, and every step is audited.
