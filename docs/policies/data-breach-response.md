# Data Breach Response Plan (draft)

A personal data breach is any unauthorised processing, accidental disclosure, access, change, loss or destruction of personal data that compromises its confidentiality, integrity or availability (DPDP Act s.2(u)).

## Timeline

| When | What | Law |
|---|---|---|
| Within 6 hours of noticing | Report the cyber incident to CERT-In | CERT-In Directions 2022 |
| Without delay | Initial intimation to the Data Protection Board of India | DPDP Rules 2025 r.7(2)(a) |
| Without delay | Tell each affected person what happened, the likely consequences, what we're doing and what they can do, and give a contact | DPDP Rules r.7(1) |
| Within 72 hours (or longer if the Board allows) | Detailed report to the Board: facts, cause, mitigation, people notified | DPDP Rules r.7(2)(b) |
| Within 72 hours | Notify the lead supervisory authority, if EU or UK members are affected and there is a risk to them | GDPR / UK GDPR Art. 33 |
| Without undue delay | Tell affected EU/UK members, if the risk is high | GDPR Art. 34 |
| As each state requires | Notify US residents and regulators | State breach laws |

The DPDP duties apply from 13 May 2027, but we follow them now.

## Steps

1. Incident response steps 1–5.
2. **Scope.** Which data, which people, which countries. Field encryption limits what an attacker can read; note whether keys were exposed.
3. **Decide** notifications with the lawyer, using the table above.
4. **Notify** in plain language: no blame, no minimising, and concrete steps (change password, watch for scam messages, use 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in).
5. **Record** every breach, notified or not, with facts, effects and actions (GDPR Art. 33(5)).

## Message template (members)

> On [date] we found that [what happened]. It affected [what data] for [you / some members]. We have [action taken].
> What you can do: [steps]. We're sorry. Questions: [Grievance Officer contact].
